Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Data We Collect

We collect the information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, and the expense records you enter — including amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions.

How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to provide the core features of Spendly: storing your expenses, generating summaries, and displaying your spending patterns back to you. We do not sell or rent your personal data to third parties.

Data Storage

Your expense data is stored securely in our database and is only accessible through your authenticated account. We take reasonable measures to protect your data from unauthorized access.

Third Party Services

Spendly does not share your personal expense data with third-party services for advertising or marketing purposes. Any third-party tools we use are limited to essential infrastructure, such as hosting.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us through the contact details provided on our website.
